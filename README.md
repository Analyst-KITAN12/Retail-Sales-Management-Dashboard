# DSN Retail Sales Analysis Dashboard

## 📊 Project Overview

This project presents an interactive **Retail Sales Management Dashboard** developed as part of my data analysis journey. The dashboard provides a visual overview of retail business performance, covering revenue, product pricing, discounts, transaction channels, geographical distribution, and monthly trends.

The goal of this project is to transform raw retail data into meaningful information that supports business monitoring, performance evaluation, and data-driven decision-making.

By combining key performance indicators (KPIs), summary tables, and visualizations, the dashboard helps stakeholders understand different aspects of retail operations and identify areas that may require further investigation.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

The main objectives of this project are to:

- Analyze overall retail revenue and key financial indicators.
- Monitor average product discount percentages.
- Examine revenue distribution across product categories.
- Understand transaction patterns across different sales channels.
- Compare transaction volumes across states.
- Analyze monthly transaction trends.
- Examine product returns using return-status information.
- Present business data in a clear, organized, and interactive dashboard.
- Support informed business decisions through data visualization.

## 📌 Dashboard Features

### 1. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

The dashboard highlights important business metrics at the top of the report.

| KPI | Displayed Value |
|---|---:|
| Total Revenue | Approximately 48M |
| Average Discount Percentage | 8% |
| Sum of Unit Price (NGN field) | 20.244M |
| Sum of Cost Per Unit | 12M |

These indicators provide a quick overview of selected financial and operational measures.

*The dashboard contains some currency-formatting inconsistencies. The monetary values and currency symbols should be standardized before using the figures for formal financial reporting.*

### 2. Revenue Analysis by State

This section compares average reported revenue across states, including Abuja, Ibadan, and Kano.

**Business purpose:**
- Compare revenue performance across locations.
- Identify geographical differences in reported revenue.
- Support further investigation into regional sales performance.

### 3. Revenue by Product Category

A summary table presents reported revenue across product categories, including accessories and other product groups.

**Business purpose:**
- Understand how reported revenue is distributed across product categories.
- Compare the contribution of different product groups.
- Identify categories that may require further sales or profitability analysis.

### 4. Sales Channel and Return Analysis

The dashboard displays sales channels alongside return-status information, including In-store and Online transactions.

**Business purpose:**
- Compare transactions across different sales channels.
- Examine the distribution of returned and non-returned transactions.
- Support further analysis of customer purchasing and return patterns.

### 5. Monthly Revenue Summary

A monthly summary table presents reported revenue for individual months, including January, February, and March.

**Business purpose:**
- Compare reported revenue across months.
- Identify changes in monthly sales performance.
- Support periodic business performance reviews.

### 6. Discount Analysis by Product Category

A column chart compares the sum of discounts across product categories such as Electronics, Home, Accessories, and Accessory.

**Business purpose:**
- Compare total discounts across product groups.
- Identify categories receiving larger discount amounts.
- Support further evaluation of promotional strategies.

### 7. Monthly Transaction Trend

A line chart shows the count of dates by month from January through August.

**Business purpose:**
- Observe changes in monthly transaction-date counts.
- Identify peaks and declines in the displayed trend.
- Support further investigation into monthly activity patterns.

**Important:** This visualization measures the count of dates, not monthly revenue. It should not be interpreted as a revenue trend.

### 8. Transaction Distribution by State

A column chart compares transaction counts across locations, including Lagos, Kano, Abuja, Ibadan, and Port Harcourt.

**Business purpose:**
- Compare transaction activity across states.
- Identify locations with higher or lower transaction counts.
- Guide further investigation into geographical sales patterns.

### 9. Transaction Distribution by Sales Channel

A column chart compares transaction counts across In-store, Online, and WhatsApp channels.

**Business purpose:**
- Understand how transactions are distributed across sales channels.
- Compare the relative volume of transactions by channel.
- Support decisions about channel performance monitoring.

## 🔍 Key Observations

Based on the dashboard visualizations:

- **Revenue overview:** Total revenue is displayed at approximately 48M, while the revenue summary table shows a total of 47,500,870.
- **Discounts:** The average discount percentage is displayed as 8%.
- **Product categories:** Electronics and Home show relatively high total discount values in the displayed chart.
- **Geographical activity:** Lagos has the highest transaction count among the states shown in the state-level chart.
- **Sales channels:** In-store has the highest transaction count among the three displayed channels.
- **Monthly activity:** The count-of-date trend fluctuates across the months, with a visible increase in July followed by a decline in August.

These observations describe the displayed dashboard. Additional analysis would be required to establish causes, profitability implications, or statistically significant trends.
